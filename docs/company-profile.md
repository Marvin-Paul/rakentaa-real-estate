## **1.  Who We Are** 

Rakentaa Real Estate is a full-service property and construction company built on a simple promise: you dream it, we build it — anywhere. From the first sketch on paper to the final coat of paint on a finished home, we walk with our clients through every stage of bringing a property to life. Our name reflects our purpose: to construct not just buildings, but lasting value for the families, investors, and businesses we serve. 

We bring together architects, quantity surveyors, project managers, valuers, and skilled site teams under one roof, so that clients no longer need to juggle multiple contractors and consultants to get a project done. Whether the need is a single-family home in a quiet suburb, a multi-unit residential development, a commercial building, or ongoing management of an existing property portfolio, Rakentaa provides one accountable point of contact from concept to completion. 

Our approach is deliberately hands-on. We believe that great buildings are the product of great relationships — between client and designer, between designer and builder, and between the property and the people who will eventually live or work in it. That belief shapes how we listen, how we plan, and how we deliver. 

#### **Our Mission** 

To turn our clients' property ambitions into safe, well-designed, and durable realities — delivered on time, within budget, and to the highest standard of workmanship, wherever our clients need us to build. 

#### **Our Vision** 

To be the most trusted name in real estate development and construction management in the region — recognised for integrity, quality craftsmanship, and the ability to deliver complex projects anywhere our clients call home. 

#### **Our Core Values** 

- Integrity — transparent costing, honest timelines, and straight communication at every stage. 

- Quality craftsmanship — every drawing, foundation, and finish held to a professional standard. 

- Client-centred design — we build around the client's life, budget, and long-term goals, not a fixed template. 

- Accountability — one team, one point of contact, one standard of delivery from start to finish. 

- Reach — the ability and willingness to deliver projects in any location our clients require.

## **4.  How We Work** 

Regardless of which service a client engages us for, we follow a consistent, disciplined process. This structure is what allows us to promise delivery 'anywhere' with confidence — the process travels with us, even when the site does not. 

- **Consultation & Briefing — we listen first: budget, site, purpose, and timeline are established before any design or costing begins.** 

- **Design & Planning — architectural drawings, structural input, and cost estimates are developed and agreed with the client.** 

- **Approvals & Procurement — permits are secured and contractors, materials, and suppliers are sourced and vetted.** 

- **Construction / Execution — work proceeds on site under continuous project management, with regular client updates.** 

- **Quality Control & Handover — inspections, snagging, and final sign-off ensure the finished property meets specification.** 

- **Ongoing Management — for clients who choose it, we continue to manage and protect the asset after handover.**
